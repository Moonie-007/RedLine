Red Line
A cozy, retro storytelling game where you are Earth, and three Mars robots text you their stories.

Open a late-90s desktop, click the Messaging app, and chat with Ingenuity, Spirit and Opportunity. They tell their missions one moment at a time, with jokes, a few tears and photos from Mars. Reply with a tap or type your own question and the rover answers.

Features
Three rovers, three personalities. Ingenuity is a tiny, shy overachiever. Spirit is gentle and sleepy. Opportunity is stubborn and deadpan.
Story told as text messages. Each message shows the mission date, like [Feb 18, 2021].
Ask anything. Type a question and the rover gives a short answer. Tap the small (i) for the longer explanation.
Photos in the chat. Mars photos arrive with the story. Click a photo to enlarge it.
Retro desktop. A late-90s desktop with a sky-and-hill wallpaper, a taskbar with a Start menu and clock, and screen grain and scanlines.
Apps on the desktop. Messaging, an Album with all the photos, a Recycle Bin and a few empty files.
Early social-network chat look, with profile pictures, an inbox and unread badges.
Cozy sound, made live in the browser: soft chimes and a music-box tune. Use Sound in the chat to turn it off.
How to run
Keep all the files together in one folder.
Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
Click Messaging, pick a rover and start chatting.

No installation, build step or server is needed. An internet connection is only used to load the pixel fonts. Without it, the game falls back to a standard console font.

Project structure
index.html   the page structure
style.css    the retro look
script.js    the story, chat logic, windows, sounds and wallpaper
images/      profile pictures and Mars photos
How to play
Click the Messaging icon on the desktop.
Choose a rover from the inbox.
Read its messages, then reply with a button or your own words.
Press Keep going, I’m listening whenever you want the next moment.
Tap (i) next to a message for a quick fact.
Make it yours
Profile pictures: click any profile box to choose a new image.
Your own photos: the Album has two empty frames you can fill.
Swap the photos: replace the files in images/ and keep the same file names.
Edit the story: the rover messages, replies, dates and facts are in the R list at the top of script.js.
Good to know
AI answers: the rovers’ answers to typed questions use an AI feature that only exists on the claude.ai page. In a copy on your own computer, a typed question gets a "the signal wobbled, say that again?" reply. The story, photos and (i) facts still work.
Facts: the dates and facts were written from general knowledge, not fetched live. Please check them against NASA’s mission pages before sharing.
Photos: the Mars photos belong to their original owners (for example NASA/JPL-Caltech). Check their usage terms before publishing the game.
Built with

HTML, CSS and plain JavaScript, plus the browser’s Canvas, Web Audio, SVG and file APIs. Fonts: Press Start 2P and VT323 from Google Fonts. No frameworks and no sound files.
